Unclaimed Tales Studio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. What we collect. When you connect TikTok, the app receives your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and an access token, as allowed by the scopes you approve (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

2. How we use it. Only to show which account is connected and to upload the videos you approve to that account. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising.

3. Where it is stored. The access token is stored only on the team's own computer, in a local settings file. It is never put in shared folders, code repositories or third-party services.

4. How long. We keep the token until you disconnect the account; then it is deleted. You can also revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions > Apps).

5. Children. The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

6. Your rights. You can ask us to delete any data we hold about you at the contact below.

7. Contact. factssow@gmail.com
